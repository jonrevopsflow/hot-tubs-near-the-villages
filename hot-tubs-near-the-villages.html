A few things you might be wondering

Good questions.
Clearer choices.

I'm just starting to look. Is this for me?

Yes. The finder is a planning tool. You don't need a model or installation date in mind.

How do I know which size will fit?

Measure your intended space and delivery route. Ask your installer to confirm the base, electrical needs, service clearance, and any required approvals before choosing a model.

Lounger or open seating?

A lounger gives you a reclined seat. Open seating offers more upright places to sit. Trying a model in person can help you compare comfort.

What should I include in my budget?

Consider the hot tub, delivery, site preparation, electrical work, accessories, and ongoing care. Finder budget ranges are preferences, not price quotes.
